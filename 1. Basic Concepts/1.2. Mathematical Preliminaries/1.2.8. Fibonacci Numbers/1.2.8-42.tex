Assume expansion exists for both \( m \) and \( n \).

\( m = F_{k_1} + \cdots + F_{k_r} \)

\( n = F_{k_1+1} + \cdots + F_{k_r+1} \)

\( mF_{N-1} + n F_N = \sum_{i=1}^{r} (F_{k_i}F_{N-1} + F_{k_i+1}F_N) = \sum_{i=1}^{r} F_{k_i+N} \)

\begin{equation}\label{eq:1}
mF_{N-1} + n F_N = \sum_{i=1}^{r} F_{k_i+N}
\end{equation}

Fibonacci expansion. Which we know is unique.

\( \implies \) original expansion is unique.

\( m\hat\phi^{N-1} + n\hat\phi^{N} \to 0, N \to \infty \implies \exists N: |m\hat\phi^{N-1} + n\hat\phi^{N}| < \phi^{-2} \)

Consider Fibonacci expansion s := \( mF_{N-1} + n F_N = \sum_{i=1}^{r} F_{k_i+N} \)

\( f(x) := \lfloor x + \phi^{-1}\rfloor \)

1.2.8-41 \( \implies f(\phi s) = \sum_{i=1}^{r} F_{k_i+N+1} \)

\( \phi s = m\phi F_{N-1} + n\phi F_N = m(F_{N} - \hat\phi^{N-1}) + n(F_{N+1} - \hat\phi^{N}) = mF_{N} + nF_{N+1} - (m\hat\phi^{N-1} + n\hat\phi^{N}) \)

\( f(\phi s) = \lfloor mF_{N} + nF_{N+1} - (m\hat\phi^{N-1} + n\hat\phi^{N}) + \phi^{-1} \rfloor = \)

\( = mF_{N} + nF_{N+1} + \lfloor \phi^{-1} - (m\hat\phi^{N-1} + n\hat\phi^{N}) \rfloor \)

\( y \geq \phi^{-1} - |m\hat\phi^{N-1} + n\hat\phi^{N}| > \phi^{-1} - \phi^{-2} > 0 \)
