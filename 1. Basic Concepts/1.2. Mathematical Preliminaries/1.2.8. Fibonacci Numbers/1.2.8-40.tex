Let's prove by induction: \( f(n) = m \iff F_{m} < n \leq F_{m+1} \)

\( n = 1: f(1) = 0, F_0 < 1 \leq F_1 \)

\( n = 2: f(2) = \min_{k=1} \max(1+f(1),2+f(1)) = 2, F_2 < 2 \leq F_3 \)

\( n \geq 3 \)

\( F_{m-1} < F_m \leq F_m \implies f(F_m) = m - 1 \)

\( n - F_m \leq F_{m+1} - F_m = F_{m-1} \)

\( f(n - F_m) \leq m - 1 - 1\)

\( f(n) \leq \max(1 + f(F_m), 2 + f(n - F_m)) \leq m \)

Assume \( f(n) < m \)

\( \exists k, 1 \leq k \leq n - 1: \max(1 + f(k), 2 + f(n - k)) \leq m - 1 \)

\( 1 + f(k) \leq m - 1 \implies f(k) \leq m - 2 \)

\( 2 + f(n - k) \leq m - 1 \implies f(n - k) \leq m - 3 \)

\( k \leq F_{f(k)+1} \leq F_{m-1} \)

\( n - k \leq F_{f(n-k)+1} \leq F_{m-2} \)

Adding last two inequalities we get:

\( n \leq F_{m-1} + F_{m-2} = F_{m} < n \implies \perp \)

\( \implies f(n) = m \)
